2. What Lotte does, and what it is not

Lotte is an endometriosis tracking companion. Every evening, it lets you log your pain, where it hurts and what surrounds it — sleep, stress, mood, food, your period, what you took —, keep track of your cycle, your medications and your flare-ups, keep a written or spoken private journal, and then read back what keeps coming up in your own history. It prepares a report to show your doctor. SOS mode is there for you during a flare-up. The optional Baby project space estimates your fertile window; the IVF space helps you follow your fertility treatment and remember everything your clinic prescribed.

Lotte is not a medical device. It makes no diagnosis, predicts nothing and gives no treatment recommendation. Triggers and “what keeps coming up” are observations about the data you entered, not a diagnosis; they replace neither the advice nor the follow-up of a healthcare professional. Unusual pain, fever or feeling faint: call 112 anywhere in the European Union, or your local emergency number.

  • Baby project gives estimates based on your cycles. Lotte is neither a method of contraception nor a fertility test: never use it to avoid a pregnancy.
  • In the IVF space, Lotte reminds you of your medications and appointments as your clinic prescribed them and as you copied them in. It never suggests, calculates or changes a dose: if in doubt, your clinic has the final word.
  • SOS mode suggests comfort measures during a flare-up; it does not replace medical care.
  • The doctor report is designed to feed the conversation with your doctor, not to replace it. Lotte makes no numerical promise of pain reduction.

Everything you enter in Lotte is stored on your phone only. The publisher has no server, no copy and no access: the detail is set out in the privacy policy. That architecture has one direct consequence:

The backup is yours to keep. Lotte is part of your phone’s iCloud or Google backup; if you lose your phone, reset it or uninstall Lotte without an up-to-date backup or an export, your history is lost and the publisher cannot recover it. On Android, the size of each app’s Google backup is capped: with many voice notes, Lotte’s backup may stop happening.
